The local spot for a proper shared meal.

GINGER AND SPICE is a Chinese restaurant at 110 Hibberson St, Gungahlin ACT, open every day for lunch (11 am–2:30 pm) and dinner (5–9:30 pm, or until 10 pm on Fridays and Saturdays), and closed on public holidays. Local dining guide

GINGER AND SPICE FAQ.

Quick answers for guests searching for Chinese food, takeaway, delivery or a table in Gungahlin.

Where is GINGER AND SPICE in Gungahlin?

GINGER AND SPICE is at 110 Hibberson St, Gungahlin ACT 2912, in Gungahlin Square near shops, parking and light rail.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day for lunch from 11 am to 2:30 pm and for dinner from 5 pm. Dinner finishes at 9:30 pm Sunday to Thursday and at 10 pm on Friday and Saturday. We are closed on public holidays.

Are you open on public holidays?

No. GINGER AND SPICE is closed on public holidays.

Do you offer takeaway and delivery?

Yes. Order takeaway online, or choose delivery through FoodHub and HungryPanda.

Can I book a table?

Yes. Call 02 6253 8721 or 0468 854 518 to book for family dinners, groups and special occasions.

What dishes is GINGER AND SPICE known for?

Dumplings, Peking duck, Szechuan dishes and banquets are highlights of the menu. The full menu has more than 120 dishes, including noodles, fried rice, seafood, curry and laksa.

Do you have banquets or set menus for groups?

Yes. There are four banquets from $26 to $45 per person: Tasty Banquet ($26, minimum 4 people), and Dinner ($31), Special ($38) and Luxury ($45) banquets for a minimum of 6 people. Call 02 6253 8721 to book a group table.

Do you serve Peking duck?

Yes. Peking Duck with Pancakes is $34.80. The menu also has Crispy Skin Roast Duck, Boneless Fried Duck and Shan Tung Duck.

Do you have spicy Szechuan food?

Yes. The Szechuan section includes Hot and Spicy Fish in Soup, Ma Po Tofu, Szechuan Kung Po Chicken and Dry Fried Chilli Chicken, and the menu rates each spicy dish from one to three chillies.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

Yes. The menu has a Vegetarian section, including Fried Eggplant, Potato and Capsicum, Spicy Cauliflowers in Dry Pot and Deep Fried Bean Curd, plus vegetable dumplings, vegetarian fried rice and a vegetarian bean curd laksa.

Do you have gluten free options?

Some dishes can be made gluten free on request, such as Sang Choy Bao and Chicken Sweet Corn Soup; they are marked on the menu. Please tell our staff about any allergy when you order.

Which dishes contain peanuts?

Dishes and sauces that contain peanuts are marked on the menu, including Sang Choy Bao and the Satay, Mongolian and Kung Po sauces. Please tell our staff about any allergy when you order.

Do I need to order lobster or mud crab in advance?

Yes. Lobster and mud crab need to be reserved two days in advance and are priced at market price. Choose ginger and shallot, hot chilli or garlic sauce.
