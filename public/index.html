GINGER AND SPICE FAQ.

Quick answers for guests searching for Chinese food, takeaway, delivery or a table in Gungahlin.

Where is GINGER AND SPICE in Gungahlin?

GINGER AND SPICE is at 110 Hibberson St, Gungahlin ACT 2912, in Gungahlin Square near shops, parking and light rail.

Do you offer takeaway and delivery?

Yes. Order takeaway online, or choose delivery through FoodHub and HungryPanda.

Can I book a table?

Yes. Call 02 6253 8721 or 0468 854 518 to book for family dinners, groups and special occasions.

What dishes is GINGER AND SPICE known for?
